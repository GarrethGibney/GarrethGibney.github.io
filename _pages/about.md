I am enviromental and labour economist studying how growing environmental risks, such as climate change, affect work and labour outcomes, and how workers and firms adjust. My research combines causal inference, microeconomic theory, and wide data sources such as surveys, administrative records, satellite data, and metrological records. 

I am currently a PhD Candidate in Economics at the University of Galway, and Teaching Assistant at the University of Limerick.
